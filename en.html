About Us

Forging steel since 1975.

Founded in 1975, Kumbasar Çelik Dövme produced hand tools by classical methods until 1990. With the investments we made that year, we became a contract and series-production supplier of forged steel parts to the construction, automotive, agricultural spare parts and machinery industries.

We're a mid-sized company with an annual forging capacity of 2,000 tons, and we keep growing. Our in-house die-revision workshop continuously renews our production dies — our principle is to meet customer needs quickly, without compromising on quality.

Production Process

From raw steel to finished part.

Every batch goes through the same three stages — cutting and preparation, hot forging, and cold forming.

01

Cutting & Preparation

Cutting, bending and slitting stainless and mild steel sheet in progressive dies.

Sizes from 10 g
02

Hot Forging

Forging heated steel into shape with hydraulic presses, a friction press and a drop hammer.

Up to 10 kg on the friction press
03

Cold Forming

Precision cold-forming of steel on hydraulic presses.

Weights from 50 g
