What we do

From forging to sheet work, every stage of the part under one roof.

Hot forging

Heated steel and iron are closed-die forged on friction and hydraulic presses; open-die work is done on the power hammer.

1,000 t friction press · parts up to 10 kg

Cold forming

Pressing and forming on hydraulic presses without heating, for tight dimensional accuracy and less machining.

40, 100 and 150 t hydraulic presses · parts from 50 g

Sheet cutting and bending

Cutting, punching, bending and slitting of stainless and black sheet with progressive dies, several operations in one stroke for series production.

Stainless and black sheet · parts from 10 g

Die making and repair

We make and maintain forging and cutting dies in our own die shop, so production does not wait when a die wears.

Lathe, milling, EDM and saw

How contract forging works

The same team works with you from the first call to shipment.

  1. Drawing or sample

    Send a technical drawing, a sample part or a photo, with material, quantity and tolerances.

  2. Review and quote

    We decide whether the part is hot or cold formed and on which press, then give you a price and lead time.

  3. Die

    We machine the die in our own die shop.

  4. Sample approval

    We measure the first parts and send them to you. Series production starts only with your approval.

  5. Production and shipment

    We produce and deliver in the agreed batches.

About

Kumbasar Çelik Dövme was founded in 1975 in Ümraniye, Istanbul, as a hand tool workshop. With new presses in 1990 we began supplying contract and series forged parts to the construction, automotive, agricultural spare parts and machinery industries.

Today we are a mid-sized forge with 2,000 tons of annual capacity that makes its own dies. The people who take your order, machine the die and run the press work under the same roof.

Industries we serve

  • Automotive suppliers
  • Construction fasteners and fittings
  • Agricultural machinery parts
  • Machine building
  • Hand tools
